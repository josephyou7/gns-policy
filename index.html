Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 18, 2026

GNS North America ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our automated HR attendance text messaging service.

1. Information We Collect

We collect mobile information such as phone numbers, text messages sent by employees (e.g., attendance and absence reasons), and time logs when interacting with our automated system.

2. How We Use Information

Collected information is used exclusively for internal employee attendance tracking, human resources management, and communicating work schedule updates.

3. SMS / Text Messaging Compliance

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the categories above exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

4. Opt-Out and Help

Employees can opt-out of the text messaging service at any time by replying STOP. For assistance, reply HELP or contact your HR department.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact GNS IT and HR Department.
